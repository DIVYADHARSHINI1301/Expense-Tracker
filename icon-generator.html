💰 ExpenseTracker App Icon Generator

📱 How to Use:

  1. Click "Download" button below each icon size
  2. Save all icons to your public/ folder
  3. Build your app: npm run build
  4. Deploy to hosting (Vercel/Netlify/Firebase)
  5. Open the deployed link on your mobile
  6. Tap "Add to Home Screen" in browser menu
  7. Enjoy your beautiful app icon! 🎉
